## Vantagens Competitivas do XForge

### Exclusivos (não existem no Radzen)

- **Latam (14)**: CPF, CNPJ, CEP, Boleto, NFe, Sped, Pix, CFOP, CNAE, NCM — conformidade fiscal brasileira
- **LGPD (6)**: ConsentBanner, CookieConsent, DataDeleteRequest, DataExportRequest, PrivacyPolicy, AnonymizationHelper
- **Editors avançados**: Spreadsheet (35 toolbar), BlockEditor, DocumentEditor, ImageEditor
- **Charts avançados**: Candlestick, Funnel, Heatmap, OrgChart, Radar, WordCloud
- **CrossPlatform**: DeviceBridge, GestureHandler, OfflineStorage, ResponsiveContainer
- **Emoji**: FluentEmoji, SkintonePicker

### Feature Parity Gaps (mesmo componente, XForge mais fraco)

| Componente | XForge | Radzen | Ação |
